Operational Architecture

How LifeLine Handles Problems

Clear, dedicated resolution pathways for digital infrastructure, campus facilities, and critical safety hazards.

💻 Controlled Digital Recovery

Digital Services

Website, Wi-Fi, ERP, LMS
Detect → Analyze → Safe Test → Approve → Verify
  • Detects HTTP outages and network probe failures automatically
  • Groups concurrent student reports from same hostel or network node
  • Performs sandbox dry-runs and network config simulation
  • Verifies live HTTP 200 and reachability before closing incident
🏢 Authority-Led Resolution

Campus Facilities

Plumbing, electrical, hostel maintenance
Report → Route → Assign → Track → Resolve
  • Routes complaints directly to designated maintenance department
  • Generates official departmental work orders with priority level
  • Assigns duty technicians and tracks on-site SLA response windows
  • Escalates unresolved delays automatically to administration
⚠️ Urgent Human Response

Safety & Health

Food safety, water issues, urgent hazards
Report → Prioritize → Escalate → Respond → Track
  • Flags health and safety risks as immediate P1 Critical priority
  • Preserves student photos, batch details, and timestamped evidence
  • Dispatches instant alerts to Food Safety and Health Officers
  • Requires formal on-site inspection and verified sign-off
