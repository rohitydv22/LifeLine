Self-healing operations for your hostel — with a human always in the loop.

LifeLine correlates what students report with facility signals, simulates recovery playbooks in a sandbox before anything touches real systems, ranks risk, and routes every high-impact action to a warden for explicit approval.

Report

Students log in, pick a category, describe what happened and where, and attach a photo — in under a minute.

Sandbox & Rank

Every report runs through a live sandbox console that correlates signals, simulates a recovery playbook, and computes a risk score — before anything is executed for real.

03

Approve

Low-risk fixes are logged for routine action. Medium and high-risk recommendations wait for a warden's explicit approval, with a full audit trail.

Built for continuity, even when sensors go dark

LifeLine is designed to keep producing useful diagnostics even when some sensors or services are unavailable — degrading gracefully instead of failing silently, so wardens always have something actionable to work with.
